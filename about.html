About Pack Math

Pack Math is a trading card pack odds and probability calculator designed to help collectors make informed decisions about their purchases.

Our Mission

We aim to provide transparent, accurate information about trading card products, helping collectors understand the true odds of pulling their favorite cards.

How It Works

Pack Math aggregates product information, box configurations, and card checklists from various trading card manufacturers. We then calculate and display the odds for different card types, inserts, and parallels.

Data Sources

Our data is compiled from official manufacturer information, verified product releases, and community contributions. We strive to keep our database as accurate and up-to-date as possible.
